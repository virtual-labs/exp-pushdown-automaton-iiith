### Procedure

1. Please go through the theory to understand the formalisms of Pushdown Automata (PDAs). Try to differentiate these from the finite automata that we saw earlier.
2. Once you are well acquainted with the theoretical aspects of PDAs, you may head to the simulation section of this experiment. Here, you would see a pushdown automata (presented visually along with its transition table), an input string, and some controls. Please follow the instructions at the top of the page to help you use the controls. This simulation helps you understand the notion of "string acceptance by a pushdown automaton". 
3. We strongly advise you to do the pre- and post-tests to gain more familiarity with the concepts.
